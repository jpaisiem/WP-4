Chapter 1

Traveling back in time, having a feeling of being transported to another world, gaining knowledge can affect your life along with others.

All these are made possible because of writing. Hello I am Michael Paisie, a writer like everyone who has the ability to write.

I have worked hard to improve my writing. Since taking CCP classes in highschool writing has been a major part of my life which I have worked to improve on.

From the start of Junior year I have had to really criticize myself and others. This was new to me and as such my editing skills were not great.

Neither were my edits, I was often harsher than I needed to be or not harsh enough.

The article Twenty-Five Pieces of Advice From Famous Writers That Are Actually Useful claims Nothing will paralyze you more as a writer than your own manuscript.

We are our own worst critics(Lavender). And she was writing, in fact through the whole composition 1 course my confidence as a writer was very low.

I struggled to believe I have any sort of high Level writing skills and since starting Composition 2 have grown to believe the right inspiration can turn anyone into a writer.

This ties into the point of how as writers we are accountable from ourselves alone. We must carve time for writing. No one else will do it for us(Lavender).

This was another struggle of mine to find the time to work on the writing outside of class time. I procrastinate a lot and didn't spend enough time writing my essays.

This is one of the big don'ts; do not procrastinate it will come back to get you in the end.

But do spend as much time as applicable working on improving your writing skills which can subsequently be done by writing essays.

Another thing is do not go too deep into a rabbit hole within a single sentence as stated by Marie Lavender Write short sentences. Or medium length.

Even go for longer ones, if that works with the plot.

But break up the flow so it becomes a true reading experience, and the audience doesn't get hung up on how you went into too much detail with one sentence(Lavender).

Tangents just confuse the reader and make everything more wordy.
