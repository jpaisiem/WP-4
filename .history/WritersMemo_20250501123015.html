Writer's Memo

  • What I would like to have done differently as it pertains to this project


  • I would have liked to spend more time planning things out (was difficult due to major time constraints)


  • I would have liked to spend more time adding more content and present fourth better vocabulary, tone, and diction


  • I would have liked to have gotten time to really look back at past papers and analyze them to determine what all factors I have improved on and still need to work on


  • If I had the opportunity to go back and start over I would have had figured out my plan from day one instead of changing my mind on what type of creative work I was going to after realizing their wasn't going to be enough time for me to really incorporate my original ideas


  • If I could go back and start from scratch I would have spent more time looking through articles to find my quotes.
